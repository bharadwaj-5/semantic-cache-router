# TechNova Inc. — Benefits & Compensation

## Health Insurance

TechNova offers three health insurance plans:

### Plan Comparison

| Feature | Bronze Plan | Silver Plan | Gold Plan |
|---------|------------|-------------|-----------|
| Monthly Premium (Employee) | $150 | $300 | $500 |
| Monthly Premium (Family) | $450 | $800 | $1,200 |
| Annual Deductible | $3,000 | $1,500 | $500 |
| Out-of-Pocket Maximum | $8,000 | $5,000 | $2,500 |
| Preventive Care | 100% covered | 100% covered | 100% covered |
| Specialist Visits | $50 copay | $30 copay | $15 copay |
| Emergency Room | 30% coinsurance | 20% coinsurance | 10% coinsurance |
| Prescription Drugs | Tier-based | Tier-based | $10/$25/$50 |

### Enrollment
- **Open Enrollment**: November 1–30 each year
- **New Hire Enrollment**: Within 30 days of start date
- **Qualifying Life Events**: Marriage, birth, adoption, loss of other coverage — 30-day window

### Dental Insurance
Two options available:
- **Basic**: $25/month, covers preventive at 100%, basic procedures at 80%
- **Premium**: $55/month, adds orthodontics and cosmetic dentistry at 50%

### Vision Insurance
- **Standard**: $10/month, annual eye exam + $150 frame allowance every 2 years

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

| Tenure | Annual PTO Days | Sick Days | Personal Days |
|--------|----------------|-----------|---------------|
| 0–2 years | 15 days | 10 days | 3 days |
| 3–5 years | 20 days | 10 days | 3 days |
| 6–10 years | 25 days | 12 days | 5 days |
| 10+ years | 30 days | 15 days | 5 days |

- PTO accrues monthly and can roll over up to **5 days** into the next year
- Unused PTO beyond the rollover cap is forfeited
- PTO requests require **48 hours** advance notice for 1–3 days, **2 weeks** for longer vacations
- Sick leave requires a doctor's note for absences exceeding **3 consecutive days**

## Parental Leave

- **Birth Parent**: 16 weeks paid leave at 100% salary
- **Non-Birth Parent / Adoptive Parent**: 8 weeks paid leave at 100% salary
- **Gradual Return**: Option to work 50% hours for 4 additional weeks at full pay
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption

## Retirement Plans

### 401(k)
- **Company Match**: 100% of the first 4% and 50% of the next 2% (maximum 5% company contribution)
- **Vesting Schedule**: Immediate vesting for employee contributions; company match vests over 3 years (33% per year)
- **Annual Contribution Limit**: Per IRS guidelines ($23,500 in 2026, plus $7,500 catch-up for 50+)
- **Investment Options**: 25+ funds including target-date, index, and ESG options

### Pension Plan
Available for employees with 10+ years of tenure:
- Benefit = 1.5% × years of service × average of last 3 years' salary
- Vesting: 100% after 10 years of service

## Tuition Reimbursement

- **Annual Cap**: $5,250 (tax-free) + up to $5,000 additional (taxable)
- **Eligible Programs**: Accredited degree programs, professional certifications, relevant courses
- **Grade Requirement**: B or above (undergraduate), B+ or above (graduate)
- **Service Commitment**: 2-year commitment post-completion; prorated repayment if leaving earlier
- Applications must be submitted **before** the course begins

## Employee Referral Program

- **Engineering Roles**: $5,000 bonus
- **Sales Roles**: $3,000 bonus
- **Other Roles**: $2,000 bonus
- Paid in two installments: 50% at hire, 50% after referred employee completes 6 months
- No limit on referrals per employee per year

## Gym & Wellness

- **Gym Membership**: Up to $75/month reimbursement for any fitness facility
- **Mental Health**: Free access to Calm/Headspace and 12 counseling sessions per year through EAP
- **Wellness Stipend**: $500 annual allowance for wellness activities (yoga, sports leagues, etc.)

## Expense Reimbursement

### Travel
- **Flights**: Coach class for domestic, premium economy for international flights over 6 hours
- **Hotels**: Up to $200/night domestic, up to $300/night international
- **Meals**: $75/day domestic, $100/day international (receipts required)
- **Mileage**: $0.67/mile for personal vehicle use
- **Ground Transport**: Rideshare or taxi receipts reimbursed at actuals

### General Expenses
- Expenses under **$100** can be approved by direct manager
- Expenses **$100–$1,000** require department director approval
- Expenses over **$1,000** require VP approval
- Submit through the Expense Portal within **30 days** of incurring the expense
- Reimbursement is processed within **10 business days** of approval

## Corporate Credit Card

Eligibility: Employees at **Level 5** (Senior) and above
- **Monthly Limit**: $5,000 (adjustable with VP approval)
- **Application**: Submit through the Finance Portal
- **Processing Time**: 7–10 business days
- All charges must be reconciled with receipts within 15 days
