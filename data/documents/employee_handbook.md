# TechNova Inc. — Employee Handbook

## About TechNova

TechNova Inc. is a global technology company founded in 2015, headquartered in San Francisco, California. Our mission is to empower businesses through innovative AI and data solutions. We have offices in New York, London, Hyderabad, and Tokyo with over 5,000 employees worldwide.

### Company Values
1. **Innovation First**: We encourage bold ideas and experimentation
2. **Customer Obsession**: Every decision starts with the customer
3. **Transparency**: Open communication at all levels
4. **Diversity & Inclusion**: Building a team that reflects the world we serve
5. **Sustainability**: Responsible growth for the long term

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are **9:00 AM to 6:00 PM** local time, Monday through Friday. Core collaboration hours are **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM** when all team members are expected to be available. Flexible scheduling is available with manager approval — employees may shift their hours by up to 2 hours in either direction.

## Remote Work Policy

TechNova operates on a **hybrid model**. Employees are expected to be in the office **at least 3 days per week** (Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are designated in-office days). Fully remote arrangements may be granted for specific roles with VP-level approval. Remote employees must maintain a dedicated workspace, reliable internet (minimum 50 Mbps), and be available during core hours.

### Remote Work Equipment
The company provides a one-time **$1,500 home office stipend** for remote-eligible employees. This covers desk, chair, monitor, and peripherals. Replacement requests require manager approval and are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

## Dress Code

TechNova maintains a **smart casual** dress code for day-to-day office work. For client-facing meetings and external events, **business professional** attire is expected. Engineering teams may follow a relaxed casual dress code unless meeting with external stakeholders. Fridays are designated casual days.

## Overtime Policy

Non-exempt (hourly) employees are eligible for overtime pay at **1.5× their regular rate** for hours worked beyond 40 per week. Exempt (salaried) employees are expected to manage their workload within standard hours but may occasionally need to work beyond them. All overtime for non-exempt employees requires **prior manager approval**. Compensatory time off may be offered to exempt employees at their manager's discretion.

## Internal Transfers

Employees who have been in their current role for at least **12 months** may apply for internal transfers. The process involves:
1. Discussing your interest with your current manager
2. Applying through the internal job portal
3. Interviewing with the hiring team
4. A transition period of 2–4 weeks after acceptance

Managers cannot block a transfer request but may negotiate the transition timeline. Compensation adjustments are handled by HR based on the new role's band.

## Code of Conduct

All employees must adhere to TechNova's Code of Conduct, which prohibits harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind. We are committed to maintaining a safe and inclusive workplace. Violations should be reported to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline at **1-800-TECH-ETHICS**.

## Company Events & Alcohol Policy

TechNova hosts quarterly social events, annual holiday parties, and team-building activities. Alcohol may be served at company-sponsored events with the following guidelines:
- Employees must be of legal drinking age
- Consumption should be moderate and responsible
- Employees should not drive after consuming alcohol — the company provides ride-share vouchers
- Alcohol is **never permitted** during working hours or in office spaces outside of approved events
