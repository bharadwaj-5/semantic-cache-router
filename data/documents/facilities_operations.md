## Parking

### How to Get a Parking Pass
1. Submit a request through the Facilities Portal at **https://facilities.technova.com/parking**
2. Upload your vehicle registration
3. HR approves within 3 business days
4. Pick up your RFID badge from the front desk
5. Monthly cost is deducted from payroll

Motorcycle and bicycle parking is free and available on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Meeting Rooms

### How to Book a Meeting Room
1. Open Google Calendar or the TechNova Spaces app
2. Click "Add Room" when creating an event
3. Select the building, floor, and capacity needed
4. Available rooms appear in green
5. Confirm the booking

### Room Capacities
| Room Type | Capacity | Equipment |
|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Huddle | 2–4 people | TV, whiteboard |
| Standard | 6–10 people | TV, camera, speakerphone |
| Large | 12–20 people | Dual screens, video conferencing |
| All-Hands | 50–100 people | Stage, projector, microphone system |

### Meeting Room Etiquette
- End meetings on time to respect the next booking
- Clean the whiteboard and remove trash
- Cancel unused bookings at least 1 hour in advance
- No-shows for 15 minutes → room auto-releases

3. Lost badges: report immediately to security@technova.com
4. Badge provides access to your assigned building and floors

- All visitors must be pre-registered through the Visitor Portal

- Visitors must be accompanied by their host at all times

## Emergency Procedures

### Fire Evacuation
1. **Sound the alarm** by pulling the nearest fire alarm station
2. **Do not use elevators** — take the stairs
3. **Exit calmly** using the nearest marked exit
4. **Assemble** at the designated meeting point (parking lot entrance, Building A)
5. **Floor wardens** (wearing orange vests) will sweep each floor
6. **Do not re-enter** until the all-clear is announced

### Earthquake
1. **Drop, Cover, Hold On** under a sturdy desk or table
2. Stay away from windows and heavy objects
3. After shaking stops, evacuate the building using stairs
4. Watch for aftershocks

### Medical Emergency
- Call extension **9111** or the external emergency number
- Locate the nearest AED (Automated External Defibrillator) — one on every floor near the elevator
- Do not move the injured person unless there is immediate danger
- First aid kits are located in every kitchen area

## Ergonomic Equipment

### How to Request
1. Complete the Ergonomic Self-Assessment at **https://hr.technova.com/ergo**
2. If recommended, an ergonomic specialist will visit your workspace
3. Equipment is ordered through Facilities (typical items: standing desk, ergonomic chair, monitor arm, keyboard tray, footrest)
4. Delivery and setup: 5–10 business days
5. All ergonomic equipment remains company property

### Available Equipment
- **Standing Desks**: Uplift V2, electronically adjustable
- **Chairs**: Herman Miller Aeron or Steelcase Leap
- **Monitor Arms**: Ergotron LX
- **Accessories**: Split keyboards, vertical mice, anti-fatigue mats

## Quiet Hours

The office observes **quiet hours from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM** daily in designated focus zones. During this time:
- No meetings should be scheduled in focus areas
- Phone calls should be taken in phone booths or meeting rooms
- Conversations should be kept to a minimum

## Pet Policy

TechNova allows well-behaved, leashed dogs in the office on **Fridays** (Dog-Friendly Fridays). Requirements:
- Dog must be registered with Facilities in advance
- Up-to-date vaccination records on file
- Owner is responsible for cleanup and behaviour
- Dogs are not allowed in cafeteria, meeting rooms, or server rooms
- Employees with allergies can request a pet-free zone assignment
