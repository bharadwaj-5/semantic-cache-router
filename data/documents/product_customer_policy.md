## Return & Refund Policy

### Return Window
- **Hardware Products**: 30 days from delivery date
- **Software Licenses**: 14 days from purchase (unused only)
- **Subscription Services**: Cancel anytime; refund prorated for unused period of annual plans

### Return Process
1. Initiate a return through the Customer Portal at **https://support.technova.com/returns**
2. Print the prepaid shipping label
3. Package the item in its original packaging
4. Drop off at any authorised shipping location
5. Refund is processed within **5–7 business days** of receiving the return

### Refund Conditions
- Item must be in original condition, unopened if applicable
- Hardware must include all accessories and documentation
- Customised or personalised items are **non-refundable**
- Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error
- Damaged or defective items: full refund including shipping, plus a **10% discount code** for the next purchase

### Regional Policies
- **EU Customers**: 14-day cooling-off period per EU Consumer Rights Directive, in addition to our standard policy
- **US Customers**: Standard 30-day policy applies
- **APAC Customers**: 21-day return window for hardware, subject to local consumer protection laws

## Privacy & Data Handling

TechNova processes customer data in compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and SOC 2 Type II. Customer data is:
- Encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Stored in the customer's chosen region (US, EU, or APAC)
- Retained for 90 days after account closure, then permanently deleted
- Never sold to third parties
- Accessible to customers via data export request within 30 days
